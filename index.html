Drop your statements here

Add one or more yearly or monthly statements. Overlapping periods are de-duplicated. The files are read in this browser tab and never leave your device.

No statement at hand? The demo uses a fictional 2026 account with real market prices. Download the sample CSV to see the expected format.

In IBKR: Performance & Reports → Statements → Activity → Period (Annual / Custom) → Format CSV.

AI review with Claude

Claude reads a summary of this page (holdings, weights, exposure, findings, targets) and writes a critique with concrete actions. Your name, account number and trade history are not sent.

The key stays in this tab's memory: it is never saved, never committed, and only sent to api.anthropic.com. Get one at console.anthropic.com; each review is billed to that key at Claude Opus 5 API rates, and the token count is shown with the result. No key? Copy the prompt and paste it into claude.ai.
